# Praiseworthy Roofing SEO Keyword Clusters

Transactional DFW clusters for money pages. English is primary. Use these phrases naturally in titles, H1s, FAQs, and body copy. Do not invent volume, rankings, or performance stats.

Hard constraints for every page that uses these clusters:

- Phone: `682-250-0078`
- Hours: Monday–Saturday 8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. Central Time; Sunday closed
- No invented proof: no “500+ roofs”, no “24 hour response”, no street address
- Insurance: inspection, photo documentation, and adjuster meeting only. Never claim insurance will pay. Never offer to waive a deductible.
- Roof replacement language stays conditional: recommend replacement only after a documented inspection supports that conversation.

## Shared service clusters

Each money page should target about five transactional phrases: the primary city + service query plus close variants.

### Free roof inspection + city

Primary intent: book a no-obligation inspection after weather or visible wear.

- `{city} roof inspection`
- `free roof inspection {city}`
- `roof inspection {city} TX`
- `residential roof inspection {city}`
- `schedule roof inspection {city}`

### Hail damage roof inspection / repair + city

Primary intent: confirm hail impact and document it before talking with insurance.

- `hail damage roof inspection {city}`
- `hail damage roof repair {city}`
- `hail damaged roof {city} TX`
- `roof hail damage {city}`
- `free hail inspection {city}`

### Storm damage roof + city

Primary intent: post-storm roof check after wind, hail, or heavy rain.

- `storm damage roof {city}`
- `storm damage roof inspection {city}`
- `roof storm damage {city} TX`
- `wind and storm roof damage {city}`
- `after storm roof inspection {city}`

### Roof repair + city

Primary intent: leaks, missing shingles, flashing, or localized storm wear.

- `roof repair {city}`
- `roof leak repair {city}`
- `residential roof repair {city} TX`
- `shingle roof repair {city}`
- `roofing repair contractor {city}`

### Roof replacement + city (supporting, honest)

Use only as a secondary phrase when the page explains that replacement is one possible outcome after inspection.

- `roof replacement {city}`
- `asphalt shingle roof replacement {city}`
- `when to replace a roof {city}`
- `storm damaged roof replacement {city}`
- `roof replacement vs repair {city}`

Do not promise a new roof, a claim payout, or a replacement timeline.

## City-level notes

These notes keep copy local without inventing neighborhood-level case counts.

- **Fort Worth:** Tarrant County core. Mix of older ranch and mid-century homes plus newer northwest / north Fort Worth subdivisions. Nearby mentions already used on the hub: West Fort Worth, North Fort Worth, Ridglea, Benbrook.
- **Plano:** Collin County. West and east Plano housing stock plus Richardson and Murphy borders. Storm systems that cross Collin County often look minor from the street.
- **Frisco:** Fast-growing Collin / Denton edge. Many newer composition roofs, plus Little Elm, McKinney, and The Colony borders.
- **Arlington:** Large south Tarrant city with north / south Arlington, Dalworthington Gardens, and Mansfield-border homes of mixed age.
- **North Richland Hills:** Core Mid-Cities service area. Pair with Richland Hills, Watauga, Hurst, and Keller.
- **Carrollton:** Dallas / Denton / Collin meeting point. Pair with Farmers Branch, Addison, and Plano-border neighborhoods.

## Internal linking rules

- Every city × service page links to its city hub, the free-inspection CTA, the other services in that city, and at least one related guide.
- City hubs for the six launch cities link down to their service pages.
- Guides link to relevant city hubs and, where helpful, to a matching service page.
- Do not generate extra city × service combinations beyond this matrix until these 24 pages have unique, useful copy.

## Claims checklist

Before publishing or expanding a cluster page, confirm the copy does **not** say:

- a specific number of roofs completed
- 24-hour or same-day guaranteed response
- a street address
- that insurance will cover or pay for the work
- that the company will waive a deductible
- that every storm-damaged roof needs replacement
